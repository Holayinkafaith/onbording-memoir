What's Inside

Everything your class
story needs.

This isn't a template. It's a hand-built, fully personalized digital home for your graduating class — alive with every name, every memory, every feeling from your years together.

👑
Legacy Wall
Every graduate. Their name, nickname, quote, best memory and future ambition — searchable, filterable, permanent.
🏆
Live Class Awards
Real voting. Real results. Most Likely to Succeed, Department Comedian, Silent Genius — voted on by the whole class in real time.
🕊️
Confession Wall
Anonymous messages — confessions, apologies, crushes, secrets. Curated and approved before they go live. No names. No drama.
⏳
Time Capsule
Every graduate writes a letter to their future self. Sealed digitally. Waiting. The site counts down to the day it opens.
🎵
Class Soundtrack
The songs that defined your years — playable right there on the site. Your anthems. Your era.
📸
Gallery & Timeline
Your best moments — dinner nights, exam season, first days — organized, captioned. A visual story of four years.
🔍
Class Archive — A Dedicated Page
A fully searchable, alphabetically filterable archive of every graduate. Click any name and their full profile slides open. On mobile it rises from the bottom. On desktop it glides in from the side. Every single person, remembered properly.

Ten years from now, someone will open this site and feel everything all over again. Their quote will still be there. Their memory will still be there. The confession that made them cry will still be there.
